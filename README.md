# Laho 🇳🇬

**Laho** is a Nigerian marketplace platform built to make buying and selling more accessible, connected, and convenient.

Inspired by the idea of bringing the marketplace experience closer to the user, Laho brings **marketers, sellers, and buyers together in one platform** — making it easier to discover products, generate leads, communicate with potential customers, and complete purchases directly through the inbox.

## 🚀 What is Laho?

Laho is designed for the Nigerian market, helping businesses and individual marketers reach more customers without the friction that often comes with traditional buying and selling.

Instead of simply displaying products, Laho focuses on **connection and conversion**.

Users can:

* 🛍️ Discover products and services
* 👥 Connect marketers with potential customers
* 📈 Generate and manage leads
* 💬 Communicate directly through the inbox
* 🤝 Build relationships between buyers and sellers
* 🛒 Make purchase arrangements directly from conversations
* 🇳🇬 Access a marketplace designed with Nigerian users in mind

## 🎯 Our Vision

Laho aims to become a trusted digital marketplace where **accessibility, discovery, communication, and commerce come together**.

We believe buying and selling should be simple:

**Discover → Connect → Chat → Purchase**

## ✨ Core Features

### Marketplace

Browse products and services from different marketers and sellers.

### Lead Generation

Help marketers reach interested customers and turn marketplace activity into genuine leads.

### Direct Messaging

Buyers and sellers can communicate directly through the platform's inbox.

### Purchase Through Conversation

Laho brings the purchasing journey closer to the conversation, making it easier for buyers and sellers to move from interest to transaction.

### Seller/Marketer Accessibility

Give marketers a platform where their products and services can be discovered by a wider audience.
